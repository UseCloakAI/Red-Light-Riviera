The mark

LOWTIDE is set tight in Archivo Extra Condensed Black. A waterline runs through the feet of the letters, because at low tide only the feet are under water. Below the line the letters ripple, and their reflection fades into the sea.

Do

  • Keep the waterline at the feet of the letters, about 13% up from the bottom.
  • Use the full version (ripple and reflection) on footage and dark grounds.
  • Use the one-colour version for print, merch and anything small.

Don't

  • Raise the waterline. It's low tide, not high tide.
  • Outline the letters or put a glow on them.
  • Set the logo in any other face, or stretch it to fit.

Motion

Things arrive fast and land softly, leave quickly, and move across the screen evenly. The logo rises out of the water with a small buoyant overshoot. Everything else settles without bouncing.

Enter

cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), 640ms

Menus, panels, text coming in.

Exit

cubic-bezier(.7,0,.84,0), 320ms

Anything leaving. Exits run about half as long as entrances.

Move

cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1), 800ms

Things already on screen moving somewhere else, like the logo going to the corner.

Select

cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 280ms

The gold bar and the tab underline. Quick, with no overshoot.
