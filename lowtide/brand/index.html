THE MARK

The O in LOWTIDE is a sun going under the water. The stripes are the tide coming in over it. It mirrors the Cloak wordmark, where the O is the orb.

Do

  • Set LOWTIDE in Anton, all caps.
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the sun on every side.
  • Put it on Night, the Sunset gradient or game footage with a dark scrim.

Don't

  • Redraw the sun or change how many stripes it has.
  • Outline the wordmark or add a glow to it.
  • Put it on Sand or Gold. The sun gets lost.
