# Cat

"Simple Cat" by Drummyfish (https://opengameart.org/content/simple-cat), released under CC0 1.0
(public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). `cat.obj` is the original mesh; `cat.png` is
the author's from-scratch texture (`cat_free.png`), downscaled to 128x128 for the PS1 look. The legs, tail and head
are animated in code (`game/streetlife.js`). No attribution required; credited anyway, with thanks.
